Michael’s design proposal

Michael Notes

A corporation in the Backrooms hires a research crew to investigate a layered orb known as the Egg. A whole region of the Backrooms appears to have imploded into it. The crew must work its way to the core to find out whether it caused the anomaly.

How the floors connect

  1. Climb. A portal-like doorway opens into a dangerous version of a familiar Backrooms location. Its geometry lets the entrance and exit meet the sphere at different elevations.
  2. Choose a return route. Step back through the exit to revisit the Threat Room, or explore the surrounding floor for its paired door.
  3. Open the shortcut from above. The crew can discover a gateway on the first floor early. It stays locked until someone finds its partner on the upper tier. Once open, the first floor connects unlocked tiers in a few crossings.

Prototype sequence

The corporation sets collection targets. Players carry material back to base, use the stations, and earn tools or research for the next stage.

  • Take an objective
  • Reach the layer
  • Harvest
  • Walk back and deposit
  • Unlock the next step

  1. Glass shell · collect glass

    Enter the Backrooms, pick up the complimentary hammer, and follow the premade ramp to the Egg. Shatter enough of the first-level shell to fill the company’s glass order. The company issues an auger.

  2. Factory · collect scrap metal

    Break into the factory beneath the glass. Take scrap back to Material Deposit to unlock the Research Station and the first scaffolding and bucket recipes. The next order is plastic from the Pool Slide. Keep collecting scrap for the crossing ahead.

  3. Level two · reach the Poolrooms

    Find the first-level door and clear its Threat Room. Level two has no premade ramp. Craft scaffold platforms at base with level-one scrap and build a bridge across the chasm. Break the glass to expose the Poolrooms, the first source of pool water. Mine the slide plastic to complete the order and unlock research for the jug and Reality Gun. Use the gun to phase in the shifting material blocking the next Threat Door.

  4. Office · collect PC parts

    Cross to the next floor, break the glass, and phase in the distorted layer covering the office. The shallow ring of cubicles is a scavenging stop. Raid desks and break computers for parts. Completing the PC-parts order earns skeleton-key research. Use the key at the locked door, clear the Threat Room, and pursue the next objective, the Company Analyzer.

  5. Hotel · find the way up

    Break the glass and auger into a mostly hollow maze of hotel rooms. The skeleton key opens every candidate door. Only one route advances. The other Threat Rooms exit at the previous landing. Read the floor number on the exit column to recognize a repeat. Random door placement can mean several attempts. A costly Door Detector offers a shortcut for a crew that has found enough treasure.

  6. Final floor · scan the Threshold

    The analyzer is waiting at the landing, completing the retrieval objective. Break the glass to find the same Threshold material that has surrounded the crew throughout the climb, now encased inside the Egg. Scan it to earn research for solvent and the solvent gun.

  7. Core · harvest the final material

    The final push draws on scrap metal, computer chips, jugs of pool water, and plastic. Craft the supplies and spray away the Threshold layers until the opening resembles the outer halls. Locate the final door. The proposed win condition is harvesting the inner core material on the top floor. The end event is still open.

Tools and supplies

Hammer
Breaks the initial glass shell. After researching the railing recipe, use the hammer with metal scrap to make railing.
Auger
Breaks hard material and collects debris from the factory and Poolrooms.
Solvent and solvent gun
Research unlocks the solvent and its applicator. Collected pool water goes into the solvent used to strip the Threshold.
Scaffolding
Craft flat railing or platform sections from scrap to span the chasm and reach the Egg at different elevations.
Reality Gun
Research tool that brings distorted Backrooms debris into phase so the crew can mine it or clear a blocked doorway.
Soft tool
Harvests softer material in the Office and Hotel.
Garbage bags
Small reusable carriers for glass and other solids. Most useful before the crew can make buckets.
Buckets
Carry solid material for deposit or research. The solvent notes also describe collecting pool water in buckets.
Jug
A liquid carrier that requires glass research. The Pool Slide objective unlocks the research in the prototype.
Marker and flashlight
Mark doors with the marker. Use the flashlight for a flash of light.
Metal Detector
Locates valuable treasure inside the Egg. Sell finds to fund optional purchases.
Door Detector
An expensive store upgrade for finding the onward route through the hotel.

Stations at base

Material Deposit
Hand in collected material to meet the current objective.
Research Station
Opens after the scrap-metal order. Research supplies the recipes and tools needed for later layers.
Craft Station
Turn collected supplies into progression items, including scaffold platforms and solvent.
Store
Spend treasure proceeds on extras and expensive upgrades. Treasure can fall out of the Egg, but a run may yield none. The crew can finish with the ordinary tools and door routes.

Details to settle

  • First Threat Door. What keeps the crew from finding or using it before the Pool Slide objective?
  • Water containers. Can buckets carry pool water before jug research, or are jugs required for the solvent supply?
  • Office or Hotel? The notes call the cubicle ring the Hotel once. The prototype above places it with the PC-parts order and treats the later hotel-room labyrinth as a separate stop.
  • Analyzer floor. The assignment names the fourth floor. The pickup happens on the last floor after the hotel. The floor numbering still needs to be reconciled.
  • Final door. Where does the core harvest happen in relation to the final door and the end event?

Player objectives and rewards

  1. Collect shell glass

    Break the first-level shell with the complimentary hammer and deposit enough glass to fill the company’s order.

    Reward The company issues the auger.

  2. Collect scrap metal

    Use the auger on the factory layer and bring the required scrap to Material Deposit.

    Reward Access to the Research Station and the first crafting recipes for scaffolding and buckets.

  3. Collect Pool Slide plastic

    Cross to the second tier, build a scaffold bridge to the Egg, and deposit plastic harvested from the slide.

    Reward Research unlocks for the jug and the Reality Gun. The gun phases in the obstruction at the next Threat Door.

  4. Collect office PC parts

    Use the Reality Gun to expose the office material. Search desks, break down computers, and deposit the parts.

    Reward Skeleton-key research. Once made, the key opens the locked onward door and the candidate doors in the hotel.

  5. Retrieve the Company Analyzer

    Find the hotel door that advances the crew, clear its Threat Room, and pick up the analyzer at the landing.

    Reward The crew keeps the Company Analyzer and receives the Threshold scan objective.

  6. Scan the Threshold

    Break the glass on the final floor and use the analyzer on the exposed Threshold material.

    Reward Research unlocks for solvent and the solvent gun, allowing the crew to craft the supplies for the core run.

  7. Retrieve the core

    Strip away the Threshold with solvent and harvest the final inner core material. The final door’s place in this sequence is still open.

    Reward Harvesting the core fulfills the proposed win condition. The end event is still to be designed.
