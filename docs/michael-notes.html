Michael’s design proposal

Michael Notes

A corporation in the Backrooms hires a research crew to investigate a layered orb known as the Egg. A whole region of the Backrooms appears to have imploded into it. The crew must work its way to the core to find out whether it caused the anomaly.

Floor connectionsPrototype sequenceTools and suppliesBase stationsObjectives and rewardsMaterialsThe Egg by elevationFull player journey

How the floors connect

  1. Climb. A portal-like doorway opens into a dangerous version of a familiar Backrooms location. Its geometry lets the entrance and exit meet the sphere at different elevations.
  2. Regroup at the door. All players must enter a Threat Room together. Each room has its own challenge, and travel through it is one-way. After exiting, search the new floor for its base gateway.
  3. Open the shortcut from above. The crew can discover a gateway on the first floor early. It stays locked until someone finds its partner on the upper tier. One player can unlock it for everyone. A teammate can then haul materials to base while the others keep working.

Prototype sequence

The expedition runs continuously from arrival to the core harvest, with no day resets. The corporation sets collection targets. Players carry material back to base, use the stations, and earn tools or research for the next stage.

  • Take an objective
  • Reach the layer
  • Harvest
  • Walk back and deposit
  • Unlock the next step

  1. Glass shell · collect glass

    Enter the Backrooms, pick up the complimentary hammer, and follow the premade ramp to the Egg. Shatter enough of the first-level shell to fill the company’s glass order. The company issues an auger.

  2. Factory · collect scrap metal

    Break into the factory beneath the glass. Take scrap back to Material Deposit to unlock the recipes for scaffolding, buckets, and a scrap lockpick. The next order is plastic from the Pool Slide. At the Craft Station, use some scrap to make the lockpick. Save more scrap for the scaffold bridge.

  3. Level two · reach the Poolrooms

    The crew can spot the first Threat Door early. Pick its lock with the scrap lockpick, then clear the Threat Room to reach level two. Level two has no premade ramp. Craft scaffold platforms at base with level-one scrap and build a bridge across the chasm. Break the glass to expose the Poolrooms, the first source of pool water. Mine the slide plastic to complete the order and unlock research for the plastic jug and Reality Gun. Once the crew has a jug, it can collect pool water for solvent. Use the gun to phase in the shifting material blocking the next Threat Door.

  4. Office · collect PC parts

    Cross to the next floor, break the glass, and phase in the distorted layer covering the office. The Office forms a shallow ring of cubicles and desks. Search the desks and break down computers for parts. Completing the PC-parts order earns skeleton-key research. Use the key at the locked door, clear the Threat Room, and pursue the next objective, the Company Analyzer.

  5. Hotel · find the way up

    Break the glass and auger into a mostly hollow maze of hotel rooms. The skeleton key opens every candidate door. Only one route leads out of the Hotel. Take that exit to find the Company Analyzer on the landing just beyond it. The other Threat Rooms exit at the previous landing. Read the floor number on the exit column to recognize a repeat. Random door placement can mean several attempts. A costly Door Detector offers a shortcut for a crew that has found enough treasure.

  6. Final floor · scan the Threshold

    With the analyzer in hand, break the glass to find the same Threshold material that has surrounded the crew throughout the climb, now encased inside the Egg. Scan it to earn research for solvent and the solvent gun.

  7. Core · harvest the final material

    Bring scrap metal, computer chips, plastic, and water-filled jugs back to base for the final push. Craft the solvent using the filled jugs. On the final floor, use the solvent gun to melt the Threshold down to its columns and walls. Search the exposed halls for the hidden final door. Enter it and harvest the inner core material inside.

Stations at base

All stations are available on arrival. If the crew has no usable recipe or material to deposit, the station asks them to come back later.

Material Deposit
Send a whole bag or bucket along the conveyor. It empties the container and returns it for reuse. Each order has a target quantity, still to be set. Surplus from the completing load gives no extra order credit.
Research Station
Research supplies the recipes and tools needed for later layers.
Craft Station
Send crafting materials down this station’s conveyor to build up its inventory. Order deposits and crafting stock cannot be transferred between stations. Use stored scrap to craft lockpicks and scaffold platforms. After researching solvent, bring water-filled plastic jugs here to craft it.
Store
Spend treasure proceeds on extras and expensive upgrades. Treasure can fall out of the Egg, but a run may yield none. The crew can finish with the ordinary tools and door routes.

Player objectives and rewards

  1. Collect shell glass

    Break the first-level shell with the complimentary hammer and deposit enough glass to fill the company’s order.

    Reward The company issues the auger.

  2. Collect scrap metal

    Use the auger on the factory layer and bring the required scrap to Material Deposit.

    Reward Recipes for scaffolding, buckets, and the scrap lockpick.

  3. Collect Pool Slide plastic

    Pick the first Threat Door’s lock and clear the room to reach the second tier. Build a scaffold bridge to the Egg, and deposit plastic harvested from the slide.

    Reward Research unlocks for the plastic jug and the Reality Gun. The gun phases in the obstruction at the next Threat Door.

  4. Collect office PC parts

    Use the Reality Gun to expose the office material. Search desks in the Office cubicle ring, break down computers, and deposit the parts.

    Reward Skeleton-key research. Once made, the key opens the locked onward door and the candidate doors in the hotel.

  5. Retrieve the Company Analyzer

    Find the correct route through the Hotel and clear its Threat Room. Go through the exit and pick up the analyzer on the landing just beyond it.

    Reward Picking up the Company Analyzer unlocks the objective to scan the Threshold.

  6. Scan the Threshold

    Break the glass on the final floor and use the analyzer on the exposed Threshold material.

    Reward Research unlocks for solvent and the solvent gun. Bring filled plastic jugs to the Craft Station to make solvent.

  7. Retrieve the core

    On the final floor, melt the Threshold down to its columns and walls. Find the hidden final door, enter it, and harvest the core inside.

    Reward Harvesting the core fulfills the proposed win condition. The end event is still to be designed.

Materials and clearing methods

  1. Glass shell

    Approach on the ramp or scaffold and shatter the glass with the hammer. Bag the first haul and deposit it for the auger. Each new approach starts at the shell.

  2. Factory metal

    Auger through the hard factory debris. Deposit scrap to unlock research and keep enough on hand to craft the lockpick and platforms for the next crossing.

  3. Pool Slide plastic and pool water

    Auger the slide and haul the solid plastic to Deposit. Once jug research is complete, return to the water with plastic jugs. Save the filled jugs for solvent.

  4. Distorted debris and Office furnishings

    Use the Reality Gun to bring the shifting obstruction into phase, then clear it. In the Office cubicle ring, use the soft tool on softer material, search desks, and break down computers for the PC-parts order.

  5. Hotel shell and rooms

    Hammer the glass and auger through the hard entry into the hollow Hotel. The soft tool can harvest softer furnishings. To advance, test doors with the skeleton key, clear their Threat Rooms, and check the exit columns. The correct exit leads to the analyzer.

  6. Threshold material

    Expose the Threshold with the hammer and scan it with the analyzer. At base, research the solvent and gun and use filled jugs to craft solvent. Spray away the material until the columns and walls are exposed.

  7. Inner core

    Search the exposed halls on the final floor for the hidden door. Enter it and harvest the core material to complete the run.
